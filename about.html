About CalTravel Guide

Updated August 2026 · CalTravel Guide

CalTravel Guide is an independent, informational website dedicated to helping travelers plan trips across California. Our goal is simple: to provide clear, practical, and honest guidance about one of the most diverse and beautiful places in the United States.

What We Cover

California is enormous — roughly the size of a small country — and its landscapes and experiences vary wildly within a few hours' drive. We focus on the things travelers most often plan around:

  • Golf — public courses, famous venues, and how to book across the state.
  • Wine country & food — Napa, Sonoma, Temecula and the culinary scenes of the major cities.
  • Destinations — coastal drives, national parks, beaches and iconic towns.
  • Itineraries & practical tips — routes, seasonal guidance, packing and planning advice.

Our Editorial Approach

Every article aims to be genuinely useful. We research each topic carefully, write in a plain and neutral tone, and update content when relevant details change. We do not accept payment to write favorably about any place, business or product. Recommendations are based on our assessment of what helps a traveler, not on sponsorship.

CalTravel Guide does not sell travel, book accommodation, or act as a tour operator. We are an information resource only. When you plan a trip, always verify hours, prices, and policies directly with the relevant venue, park, or business before you go.

Our Editorial Standards

Every guide on CalTravel follows a simple set of standards designed to earn your trust:

  • Independence. We are not a travel agency, tour operator, or booking service. We sell nothing and take no commissions from the places we cover. Sponsorship never influences our recommendations.
  • Accuracy. We research each topic against official and authoritative sources, and we update guides when details change. When you plan a trip, always double-check hours, prices, and policies with the official source, because specifics can shift.
  • Originality. All content is written by our editorial team. We do not recycle or rewrite articles from other publications.
  • Usefulness. We prioritize practical, actionable detail — the logistics, costs, and realities you need to plan a trip — over generic praise.
  • Transparency. Any commercial relationship or advertisement is clearly disclosed on our Advertising Disclosure page.

What We Don't Do

We will never publish paid-for positive reviews, hide sponsorship inside an article, or exaggerate a destination to drive clicks. If we can't recommend a place truthfully, we say so.

Get in Touch

We welcome questions, corrections and feedback. Please visit our contact page to reach us.
