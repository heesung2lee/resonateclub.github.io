Performing Arts in California: The Hollywood Bowl & Beyond

Updated AUGUST 2026 · GoldenPath Guide
· By GoldenPath Guide Editorial Team

On a warm summer evening, thousands of people settle onto benches beneath the great white bandshell of the Hollywood Bowl, a picnic spread at their feet and the LA Philharmonic about to play. This is California performing arts at its most iconic: a blend of world-class music, cinematic surroundings and a laid-back al fresco spirit that might be unmatched anywhere. From a legendary 18,000-seat amphitheater to glittering concert halls, the state’s performance scene rewards an evening out at almost every stop.

The Hollywood Bowl

The Hollywood Bowl is the crown jewel — a roughly 18,000-seat amphitheater tucked into the Hollywood Hills, famous from films and television and beloved locally. Every summer it hosts a long season of concerts headlined by the LA Phil, alongside orchestras, film-score nights, jazz and pop acts. The signature move is the picnic tradition: bring a hamper, arrive early, and dine at your seat as the sun dips behind the hills.

  • Book ahead. The biggest nights sell out, so reserve tickets early.
  • Al fresco energy. Evenings are mild and the open-air setting is the whole point.
  • Getting there. The Bowl runs shuttle service from the nearby Metro station on concert nights.

Greek Theatre & the LA Concert Halls

Just above the Bowl sits the Greek Theatre, a grassy, open-air venue in Griffith Park with a more intimate scale and a strong rock and pop calendar. Downtown, architect Frank Gehry’s undulating stainless-steel Walt Disney Concert Hall is a building as dazzling as the music inside it, home to the LA Phil during the indoor season. For another celebrated stage, the world-class San Diego Old Globe in Balboa Park carries on a storied tradition of live theater.

San Francisco & the Bay Area

Across the bay, San Francisco fields a deep cultural roster. The SF War Memorial complex houses both the opera and the symphony, and the city’s classical and dance seasons rival any in the West. In Oakland, the vintage Fox Theater draws touring acts and movie-related spectacles inside a gorgeously restored movie palace. On a smaller, more bohemian note, a thriving independent theater community keeps the Bay Area buzzing year-round.

Summer Festivals

California’s performance calendar peaks with its summer festivals. Outside Lands takes over Golden Gate Park in San Francisco with a sprawling lineup of music, food and wine. Coachella, in the desert east of Los Angeles, brings global headliners to the Colorado Desert for two epic weekends. Both are less about the concert hall and more about the open-sky experience the state does so well.

Planning an Evening

Nighttime in the open air is California’s signature cultural setting, and a little planning goes a long way.

  1. Book tickets in advance. Evenings at the Bowl, Disney Hall and festival weekends sell out.
  2. Dress for the evening. Al fresco venues can grow cool after sunset — bring a layer.
  3. Go early. Arrive for the picnic, tour the grounds, and soak in the setting before the downbeat.
Getting around: The Bowl and Greek Theatre are best reached with the venue shuttle or a rideshare, as parking in the Hollywood Hills is tight. City venues are typically transit-friendly.
