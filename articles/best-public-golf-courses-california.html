Best Public Golf Courses in California

Updated August 2026 · CalTravel Guide
· By CalTravel Guide Editorial Team

California famously heads the country in both the number of courses and the price of a round. But the state also owns some of the finest public golf anywhere on earth. These are the daily-fee and municipal layouts that deliver championship quality without a private membership card — the ones worth planning a trip around.

What makes a course “best value”

Value is never just a low price. We weigh the design pedigree, the condition, the views, the difficulty relative to your game, and the ease of getting a tee time. A $300 round you love can be better value than a $50 round you dislike — but for most golfers the sweet spot is a great design at a sensible price. The courses below hit that balance.

Our picks, by region

Southern California

  • Torrey Pines South (La Jolla) — the premier municipal championship anywhere, built on cliffs above the Pacific. Host of two U.S. Opens. Long, exposed and memorable, and open to the public with advance booking.
  • Torrey Pines North (La Jolla) — the friendlier sibling, renovated by Tom Weiskopf in 2016. Same jaw-dropping views, far more forgiving — ideal on a day when the South might be too much.
  • Industry Hills (City of Industry) — a two-course complex on a former landfill, with a funicular ride up to the tee. Rugged, demanding and noticeably cheaper than the coastal icons.
  • The Golf Club of California (Fallbrook) — a quiet daily-fee gem with oak groves and valley views, better known to locals than to tourists.

Central Coast & Inland

  • Rustic Canyon (Moorpark) — Gil Hanse’s minimalist masterwork, built with barely any earth moved. Strategic, walker-friendly and prized by design purists. One of the most lauded public courses in the state.
  • La Purisima (Lompoc) — a sprawling, housing-free course on the central coast. Long, windy and serious — a true test that hosted PGA Tour qualifying.
  • Alisal River Course (Solvang) — a scenic, barranca-crossing layout in Santa Ynez wine country, scenic over punishing.

Desert

  • PGA West Mountain (La Quinta) — a scenic Pete Dye layout against the Santa Rosa Mountains, more forgiving than its Stadium sibling.
  • Rams Hill (Borrego Springs) — a Tom Fazio redesign of an oasis course, open winter through spring. A serene, quiet escape.

How to choose the right tee box

California’s championship courses play far shorter in yardage than their reputations suggest — but the exposed coast, the desert grasses and the quick greens add strokes. Pick a tee set that plays comfortably for your handicap rather than chasing the color used by the professionals. On the hardest courses, moving up a tee box is the difference between a fun round and a long one.

A note on walking

Many of these courses — particularly Rustic Canyon and the Torrey Pines pair — are walkable and best enjoyed on foot, where you can take in the views and the pace of the round. Others, such as Industry Hills and the desert courses, involve long walks between greens or steep ascents, where a cart is well worth the extra cost.

Wherever you land, the state’s public options are among the best in the world. Pack a light jacket for the coast, a hat and sunscreen for the desert, and enjoy one of golf’s most varied landscapes.

Budgeting for a round

Because fees vary so much, it pays to plan around the budget. A few general pointers help most visitors:

  • Book weekday mornings. Off-peak times are cheaper and usually less crowded.
  • Consider twilight. Late-afternoon tee times can be dramatically cheaper, though you may not finish all 18.
  • Skip the premium add-ons. On walkable courses, a caddie is optional — though at the very top resorts it may be included.
  • Compare direct vs. third-party. Booking straight from the course is often the cheapest and keeps your options flexible.

Whatever you spend, the quality of California’s public golf is a big part of why people travel here. Choose the courses that suit your game and your budget, and you’ll be rewarded with some of the finest public rounds in the sport.
