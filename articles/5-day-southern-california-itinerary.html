5-Day Southern California Itinerary: Los Angeles to San Diego

Updated AUGUST 2026 · CalTravel Guide

This five-day Southern California itinerary gives you the classic coast-to-border run from Los Angeles to San Diego without feeling rushed. It balances iconic landmarks, beautiful beaches and laid-back neighborhoods, with sensible overnights that keep driving to a minimum.

Day 1: Arrive & Explore Santa Monica

Land in Los Angeles and settle into Santa Monica. Spend the afternoon on the beach and the famous Santa Monica Pier with its Ferris wheel, and walk the pedestrian-only Third Street Promenade. Catch a sunset over the Pacific.

  • Santa Monica Pier — the classic first stop.
  • Third Street Promenade — shopping and street performers.
  • Palisades Park — clifftop ocean views.

Day 2: Hollywood & Beverly Hills

Spend the day with L.A.’s icons. Walk the Hollywood Walk of Fame and the TCL Chinese Theatre, then drive to the Griffith Observatory for a sweeping view of the skyline and the Hollywood sign. Head to Rodeo Drive in Beverly Hills in the afternoon.

  1. Walk the Hollywood Walk of Fame.
  2. Take a photo op at the Hollywood sign (best from Griffith Observatory).
  3. Browse Rodeo Drive in Beverly Hills.
  4. Wrap up at The Grove or Farmers Market for dinner.

Day 3: Coast to Orange County

Drive south along the coast to Orange County. Visit Venice Beach on the way out, then explore Main Beach Laguna and the artsy town of Laguna Beach. Overnight in Huntington Beach, the self-proclaimed “Surf City USA.”

  • Venice Beach — boardwalk and street performers.
  • Laguna Beach — art galleries and scenic coves.
  • Huntington Beach — surf culture and a lively pier.
Watch the drive time: Los Angeles traffic can be brutal. Leave Santa Monica by mid-morning and plan for up to two hours to reach Orange County, especially on a summer weekend.

Day 4: North San Diego & La Jolla

Head south into San Diego County. Meet the sea lions at La Jolla Cove, explore the coves and cliffs of Torrey Pines, and drive down to the beach town of La Jolla Shores. Overnight in central San Diego.

  • La Jolla Cove — watch sea lions and seals.
  • Torrey Pines State Reserve — coastal cliff trails.
  • Balboa Park — if time permits, see its gardens in the evening.

Day 5: San Diego Highlights & Depart

Make the most of your final day. Start at the sprawling Balboa Park or the San Diego Zoo, walk the historic Gaslamp Quarter, and see the waterfront. From here you can depart from San Diego International Airport, a short trip from downtown.

  1. Morning — Balboa Park or the San Diego Zoo.
  2. Midday — Gaslamp Quarter and the waterfront.
  3. Afternoon — head to the airport for your flight home.

Tips for This Route

Rent a car — it’s the easiest way to cover the spread-out region. Book lodging ahead, especially in Santa Monica and San Diego in peak season. And stay flexible: the weather and crowds can shift plans, and that’s half the fun.
