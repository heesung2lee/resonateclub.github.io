California Travel Tips: Essential Planning Advice

Updated AUGUST 2026 · GoldenPath Guide
· By GoldenPath Guide Editorial Team

Drive south on Interstate 5 toward Los Angeles and you will see, in a single afternoon, fogbank on the coast, 100-degree heat inland, and snow caps on the Sierra far to the east. That is the first thing to understand about California: nothing about it is consistent. The good news is that a handful of decisions — when you go, how you move, what you carry — decide whether the trip feels effortless or exhausting.

This is the hub for the practical guides on this site. Each of the links below leads to a full article, so start with whatever question is keeping you up at night.

The Big Questions, Answered

Three questions come up in almost every planning conversation we have with travelers. Each has a dedicated guide.

  • When to go? Season reshapes weather, crowds and prices, and it changes dramatically from region to region. See the Best Time to Visit California guide.
  • How to get around? Public transit covers the big cities and little else. A car is the easy answer, but tolls, insurance and parking demand some know-how. Read the Rental Car Guide.
  • What to pack? Microclimates mean you pack layers no matter the month. The Packing Guide breaks it down by season and region.

Quick Planning Principles

These four rules will save you more time than any spreadsheet.

  1. Pick a region, don’t conquer the state. Distances are huge and the traffic is real.
  2. Book ahead for lodging, national park entry and any star attraction you’d be sorry to miss.
  3. Dress in layers — coastal fog, mountain cool and desert heat often coexist on the same day.
  4. Check road conditions for mountain passes and Highway 1, especially between November and April.
Start with the right season: If your dates are flexible, spring (March–May) and fall (September–October) give the best weather and the thinnest crowds across most of the state.
