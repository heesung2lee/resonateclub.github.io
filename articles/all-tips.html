California Travel Tips: Essential Planning Advice

Updated AUGUST 2026 · CalTravel Guide

California rewards a little planning. A few simple tips on timing, transport and packing make the difference between a smooth trip and a stressful one. This hub links to our most practical travel-tips guides to help you plan with confidence.

The Big Questions, Answered

Below are the three most common things travelers research before a California trip — each has a full guide.

  • When to go? Season greatly affects weather, crowds and prices, and it varies a lot by region. See the Best Time to Visit California guide.
  • How to get around? A car is the easiest way to cover the state’s spread-out attractions, but tolls, insurance and parking require a little know-how. Read the Rental Car Guide.
  • What to pack? Microclimates mean you need layers year-round. The Packing Guide breaks it down by season and region.

Quick Planning Principles

  1. Pick a region, don’t conquer the state. Distances are large and traffic is real.
  2. Book ahead for lodging, national park entry and starred attractions.
  3. Dress in layers — coastal fog, mountain cool and desert heat often coexist.
  4. Check road conditions for mountain passes and Highway 1, especially in winter.
Start with the right season: If you’re flexible, spring (March–May) and fall (September–October) offer the best balance of weather and smaller crowds across most of the state.
