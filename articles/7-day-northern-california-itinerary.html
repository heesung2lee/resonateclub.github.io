7-Day Northern California Itinerary: SF, Napa & the Big Sur Coast

Updated AUGUST 2026 · CalTravel Guide
· By CalTravel Guide Editorial Team

This seven-day Northern California itinerary takes you from the city of San Francisco through the hills of Napa wine country and down one of the world’s great coastal drives — the Pacific Coast Highway through Big Sur. It’s a route built on scenery, food and iconic landmarks.

Day 1: San Francisco

Get oriented in the city. Ride a cable car, see the iconic Golden Gate Bridge, and wander the colorful houses of Alamo Square and the bustling Fisherman’s Wharf. Take a ferry to Alcatraz if you booked ahead, and dine on fresh seafood.

  • Golden Gate Bridge — walk or cycle the span.
  • Cable cars — a ride up Nob Hill.
  • Alcatraz Island — book well in advance.

Day 2: Napa & Sonoma

Head north into wine country. Spend the day visiting Napa Valley wineries for tastings, then explore the charming town of Sonoma. Arrive early or book tastings in advance for the best experience.

  1. Morning — Napa Valley tastings.
  2. Afternoon — Sonoma Plaza.
  3. Evening — dinner in the wine country.
Designate a driver: Between tastings, arrange a driver or join a tasting tour, since driving while drinking is unsafe and against the law.

Day 3: Coast to Monterey

Drive south to the coast, stopping in Half Moon Bay and Santa Cruz. Arrive in Monterey in time for the world-famous Monterey Bay Aquarium and a walk along Cannery Row.

  • Half Moon Bay — coastal farmland and seafood.
  • Santa Cruz — the historic boardwalk.
  • Monterey Bay Aquarium — a world-class center.

Day 4: Big Sur

Today is the highlight. Drive the Pacific Coast Highway through Big Sur, stopping at Bixby Bridge, Pfeiffer Beach and the waterfall at McWay Falls. The dramatic cliffs and ocean views are unforgettable. Overnight in Big Sur or nearby Carmel.

  • Bixby Creek Bridge — a must-photo stop.
  • McWay Falls (Julia Pfeiffer Burns) — a beach waterfall.
  • Pfeiffer Beach — reserve ahead in season.

Day 5: San Simeon & the Coast

Continue south. Visit the opulent Hearst Castle at San Simeon (timed tours, book ahead), see the elephant seals at Piedras Blancas, and overnight in San Luis Obispo.

  1. Hearst Castle tour.
  2. Elephant seal rookery at Piedras Blancas.
  3. Overnight in San Luis Obispo.

Day 6: Santa Barbara

Drive to Santa Barbara, known as the “American Riviera.” Stroll the red-tile Mission Santa Barbara, relax on the beach and enjoy the Spanish-colonial architecture. It’s a perfect place to slow down.

Day 7: Return & Depart

Drive back to San Francisco (about 5 hours) or continue south toward Los Angeles if your trip continues. If flying out of SFO, budget plenty of time for the return drive and traffic.

Tips for This Route

Book ahead for Alcatraz, Hearst Castle and Big Sur lodging, all of which sell out quickly. Chains may be required in winter on mountain passes, and a section of Highway 1 occasionally closes for mudslides — check conditions before you go. And above all, take your time: the coastal views are the star.
