Fall Travel Deals: California in the Shoulder Season

Updated AUGUST 2026 · CalTravel Guide
· By CalTravel Guide Editorial Team

Good news for anyone scouting travel prices: autumn is when California's hotels roll out the deals. Across the state, destinations sweeten stays with free nights, dining credits, and spa vouchers. The shoulder season — crisp weather, fewer crowds, lower rates — is the ideal time to plan a proper trip.

Why Fall Works

California in autumn is marked by comfortable weather and thinner crowds. September is designated California Wine Month, so wineries statewide roll out discounts and special programs. October brings free children's admission in San Diego. Visit any destination's official tourism site and you'll find everything from free nights to dining credits.

  • Fewer crowds — summer's spike has eased, so sights feel manageable.
  • Wine Month — September perks across the state's wineries.
  • Free adm days — national and state parks add free-entrance days in fall.

Northern California Deals

In Napa Valley, the Silverado Resort knocks 20% off its best available rate during the season and includes a pass for discounts and 2-for-1 tastings at some 20 wineries. In Fort Bragg, the Noyo Harbor Inn discounts both a stay and a Skunk Train ticket by 15%. Up in Sonoma, Dawn Ranch and Russian River Resort offer up to 40% off for longer stays. And Sacramento downtown hotels lure you in with $1 valet parking.

Central & Southern Deals

At Pismo Beach, the Dolphin Bay Resort offers 15% off plus a $50 daily dining credit. Mammoth Mountain's fall season gives 20–25% off for stays of two or more nights. In Visalia, the Darling Hotel stacks 10% with a $30 dining credit. And both Sequoia and Kings Canyon National Parks host free-entrance days in the fall — time a visit to one and it's an especially good deal.

San Francisco Packages

At the Hyatt Regency on San Francisco's Embarcadero, the "Launch Into Space" package includes tickets to the astronaut life exhibit at the Exploratorium, while the Urban Explorer package pairs a Bay-view room with hop-on/hop-off tour tickets. For families wanting to pack in varied experiences, these bundles offer real value. When you're in the city, check each site's details to find the deal that fits you.

Tip: Compare the official tourism sites with booking platforms side by side. Most deals are limited to specific dates and days, so confirm your preferred dates are valid — and if it works out, line one up with a wine season or free-entrance day for even more value.
