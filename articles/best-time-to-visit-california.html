Best Time to Visit California: Season Guide by Region & Month

Updated AUGUST 2026 · CalTravel Guide

“When is the best time to visit California?” is the most common question travelers ask — and the answer is: it depends on where you’re going. Because of the state’s varied geography and Mediterranean climate, there’s no single best month for everyone. This guide breaks down what each season means for the coast, the mountains, the desert and the wine country.

California’s Climate in a Nutshell

California largely enjoys a Mediterranean climate: mild, wet winters and warm, dry summers. But the state is extraordinarily diverse — the coast is cool and foggy, the inland valleys get hot, the mountains see heavy snow, and the desert can be blazing in summer. Your ideal season depends entirely on your destination.

Best Time by Destination

California Coast & Big Sur

The Pacific coastline is gorgeous most of the year, but late spring to early autumn offers the driest roads and clearest days. Summer skies are often sunny, though mornings can be foggy. Winter and early spring bring rain and occasional Highway 1 closures.

Yosemite & the Sierra

Summer (June–September) is prime season, when the high country opens and waterfalls run. Spring is spectacular for peak water flow but Tioga Pass may be closed. Winter is beautiful and quiet but requires chains.

Lake Tahoe

Winter is for skiing; summer is for lakes, beaches and hiking. Fall is a lovely shoulder season with fewer crowds and gold aspens.

Southern California & San Diego

Southern California is comfortably warm and sunny much of the year. Spring and fall are ideal — warm days without summer crowds. Summer brings peak crowds and occasional marine layer; winter is mild and affordable.

Wine Country

Napa and Sonoma are wonderful in early fall, during harvest, and pleasant in spring. Summer can be hot; winter is quiet with occasional rain.

Best overall windows: If you want the most comfortable, crowd-free trip, aim for late spring (May–early June) or September–early October. You’ll get warm weather, fewer tourists and reasonable prices.

Budget and Crowds

Summer is peak season — expect the highest prices and biggest crowds at beaches, national parks and wine country. Winter and early spring offer the lowest cost for many destinations (except ski resorts), with shelter from crowds. If flexibility is your strength, travel in the shoulder seasons for the best balance of value, weather and solitude.
