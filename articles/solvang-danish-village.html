Solvang Travel Guide: Danish Village History, Wine & Family Day

Updated AUGUST 2026 · CalTravel Guide

Tucked into the Santa Ynez Valley north of Santa Barbara, Solvang is a charming village that celebrates its Danish heritage with windmills, half-timbered buildings, bakeries and strudel. It’s a quirky, family-friendly stop that pairs well with the region’s vineyards and wine country.

A Short History of Solvang

Solvang was founded in 1911 by a group of Danish settlers who wanted to establish a Danish colony in the mild California climate. The name means “sunny field” in Danish. Over the decades the town leaned into its heritage, adopting classic Danish architecture, and today it’s one of the most distinctive small towns in the state — a slice of Scandinavia on the Central Coast.

What to See & Do

The Danish-Style Downtown

Most of Solvang’s attractions sit on a compact, walkable downtown. Wander the streets to admire the half-timbered storefronts, a working windmill and a replica of Copenhagen’s Little Mermaid statue. The vibe is festive and great for photos.

Bakeries & Sweets

Solvang is best known for its Danish pastries — aebleskiver (little spherical pancakes) and freshly baked kringle are favorites. Stop at one of the traditional bakeries and enjoy a coffee on a patio.

  • Danish pastries & aebleskiver — the classic treat.
  • Chocolate and candy shops — good stocking-stuffers and souvenirs.
  • The Solvang Windmill — a local landmark.

Museums & Attractions

The Elverhøj Museum of History & Art traces the town’s Danish roots, while the Hans Christian Andersen Museum honors the famous storyteller. Families also enjoy the OstrichLand and Ostrich Farm just outside town, where you can feed ostriches and emus.

Wine & the Santa Ynez Valley

Solvang sits in the heart of a celebrated wine region. The Santa Ynez Valley is known for cool-climate Pinot Noir and Chardonnay, and there are dozens of tasting rooms within a short drive. A tasting afternoon is a lovely complement to a stroll through the village.

  1. Downtown tasting rooms — several wineries have rooms right in town.
  2. Nearby wineries — drive out to the valley’s vineyards.
  3. Pair with Los Olivos — the neighboring village, also full of tasting rooms and boutiques.
Best times to visit: Year-round weather is mild. Spring and fall are ideal for comfortable days and wine tasting. The town hosts festive events in winter (a traditional Danish Christmas market) and spring — check the calendar for food and wine festivals.

Making It a Family Day

Solvang is compact and safe to stroll, making it a relaxing stop for kids. Combine the wineries (for adults) with the ostrich farm, a pastry stop and a ride on one of the small horse-drawn or pedal vehicles around town. With its storybook look, it’s a gentle, memorable break on a Central Coast drive.

Getting There

Solvang is about a 45-minute drive north of Santa Barbara along a scenic route. It makes an easy day trip from the Santa Barbara coast, or a wonderful overnight stop on a San Francisco to Los Angeles coastal journey. Parking is on the streets or in small lots downtown.
